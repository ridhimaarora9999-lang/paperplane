✈️ Paper Plane: My Building Project

A paper plane is a simple model aircraft made by folding a sheet of paper. It is an interesting beginner-friendly project because it combines creativity, design and basic principles of aerodynamics.

About Paper Planes

Paper planes can be made in many different shapes and designs. Some designs are made to fly long distances, while others are designed to stay in the air for a longer time. Small changes in the folds can change the way a plane flies.

Materials Required

  • One A4 size sheet of paper
  • Pencil
  • Ruler
  • Scissors
  • Colours or sketch pens for decoration

How to Make a Paper Plane

  1. Take an A4 size sheet of paper.
  2. Fold the paper in half lengthwise and then open it.
  3. Fold the top two corners towards the centre line.
  4. Fold the sloping edges towards the centre again.
  5. Fold the paper along the original centre line.
  6. Fold both sides down to form the wings.
  7. Adjust the wings so that they are balanced.
  8. Throw the plane gently and observe its flight.

Paper Plane Design

The design of a paper plane affects its speed, stability and flying distance. A well-balanced plane can travel more smoothly.

Basic Aerodynamics

Four main forces affect the flight of an aircraft: lift, weight, thrust and drag. In a paper plane, the folds and shape of the wings affect how these forces act on the plane.

  • Lift: Helps the plane stay in the air.
  • Weight: Pulls the plane downward.
  • Thrust: Comes from the force used to throw the plane.
  • Drag: Air resistance that slows the plane down.

Try Different Designs

After making a basic paper plane, we can experiment with different wing shapes and folds. We can then compare which design flies farther or stays in the air longer.
